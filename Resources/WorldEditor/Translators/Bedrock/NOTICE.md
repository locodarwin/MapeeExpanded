# Bedrock translation data

Generated by `Tools/BedrockMappings/generate.py`; regenerate rather than editing by hand.

- `Blocks.json` comes from `blocksB2J.json` in [PrismarineJS/minecraft-data](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data) (MIT License).
- `Light.json` comes from `blocks.json` (Java edition) in [PrismarineJS/minecraft-data](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data) (MIT License): the light each block gives off and filters, used to compute the light Bedrock doesn't save.
- `Biomes.json` reverses `biomes.json` from [GeyserMC/mappings](https://github.com/GeyserMC/mappings) (MIT License, Copyright (c) GeyserMC), plus a hand-written table of legacy Bedrock biome IDs.
